Delete Your Account

Education News users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address registered to your Education News account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Education News account.
  3. Include your registered name and phone number so we can verify the request.
Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send the request directly to support@enreducationnews.app.

What will be deleted

  • Your Firebase authentication account.
  • Your reporter profile and personal details, including contact and identity information.
  • Private drafts and account-linked notification records where applicable.
  • Uploaded profile and article images where technically available and not required for retained public content.

Data that may be retained

Published articles, payment or accounting records, fraud-prevention records, and information required to meet legal obligations may be retained where necessary. Public articles may be anonymized instead of removed. Backup copies and hosted images may remain for a limited period before deletion is completed across all systems.

We may contact you to verify ownership of the account. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days and will confirm when the request is complete or explain any information that must be retained.

For more details, read our Privacy Policy.
