Delete Your Data

You can ask Education News to delete selected personal data associated with your account without requesting deletion of the entire account.

How to request data deletion

  1. Email us from the address registered to your Education News account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Education News data.
  3. Include your registered name and phone number for verification.
  4. Clearly list the data you want deleted, such as your profile photo, address, Aadhaar number, drafts, or uploaded images.
Request data deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send your request to support@enreducationnews.app.

Data you may request to delete

  • Profile details, contact details, address, and optional identity information.
  • Profile photos and other uploaded images.
  • Unpublished article drafts and associated media.
  • Account-linked notification and support records where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Published articles and their necessary attribution, payment or accounting records, fraud-prevention records, and information required by law may be retained. Backup copies and hosted images may remain for a limited period while deletion is completed across our systems.

We may contact you to verify ownership of the data. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days and will confirm completion or explain why specific information must be retained.

To delete your entire account, use our Account Deletion page. For more information, read our Privacy Policy.
